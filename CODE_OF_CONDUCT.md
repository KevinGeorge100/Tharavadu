# Code of Conduct

Tharavadu is a place for respectful collaboration. Treat contributors and the people represented in family stories with care. Be welcoming across backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and family structures. Critique work constructively; do not harass, threaten, demean, or disclose another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through their GitHub profiles, with a link or description of the incident. Do not include sensitive family data in a public issue. Maintainers should review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where practical, and respond proportionately.
